How it works

Three moves. You sign each one.

01

Pick the pool

Every Uniswap v3 pool on Robinhood Chain ranked by fee APR, depth and volume. Coins, USDG and tokenized stocks like NVDA and TSLA. Thin pools are flagged, not hidden.

02

Set the range

Say how much you put in and how wide: ±2% for calm pairs, ±20% for stocks, wider for new coins. Glidy sizes the other token from the live price and snaps it to the pool's ticks.

03

Glide

Watch in-range status and fees in real time. Collect in one tap. When price leaves your range, Glidy prepares the rebalance: remove, then a fresh range around the price.

The Checkpoint

No ticket until
the deposit checks out.

Every add runs the same checks first, on the live chain. If one fails, there is no ticket. If it passes, you still sign it yourself, after one more check right before signing.

FAQ

Questions

Does Glidy hold my funds or keys?

No. Glidy prepares unsigned transactions. Your wallet signs them. Your position is a Uniswap v3 NFT in your own wallet.

Which pools?

Uniswap v3 on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663): WETH, USDG, tokenized stocks and ETFs, and new coins. Any pool from the official factory works.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Prices move: impermanent loss, a range that goes out of range, and risky tokens. The Checkpoint shows these before you sign, but it is a set of checks, not a guarantee.

Do I need both tokens?

You choose one side and an amount; Glidy tells you how much of the other you need. Native ETH is used for the WETH side. Swap first in the app if you are short.
