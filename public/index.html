How it works

Three moves.
You sign every one.

01

Pick the pool

Every Uniswap v3 pool on Robinhood Chain ranked by fee APR, depth and volume. WETH, USDG, tokenized stocks and new coins. Thin pools are flagged, not hidden.

02

Set the range

Tell Glidy what you put in and how wide. It sizes the other token from the live price, snaps to the pool's ticks, and shows how long price usually stays inside.

03

Glide

Watch in-range status and fees in real time. Collect in one tap. When price leaves your range, Glidy prepares the rebalance and waits for your signature.

FAQ

Questions

Does Glidy hold my funds or keys?

No. Glidy prepares unsigned transactions and your wallet signs them. Your position is a Uniswap v3 NFT that lives in your own wallet.

Which pools can I use?

Every Uniswap v3 pool from the official factory on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663): WETH, USDG, tokenized stocks and ETFs, and new coins.

Do I need both tokens?

Choose one side and an amount; Glidy tells you how much of the other you need. Native ETH is used for the WETH side. Short on one? Swap first, right in the app.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Impermanent loss, ranges that go out of range, and risky tokens are real. The Checkpoint shows them before you sign; it is a set of checks, not a guarantee.

Is there an API?

Yes. Every tool the agent uses is a REST endpoint and an MCP tool: list_lp_pools, lp_positions, check_lp, prepare_lp_add, prepare_lp_collect and prepare_lp_remove.
